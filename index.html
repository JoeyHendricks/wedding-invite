Before you ask

Questions

What should I wear?
Day one is yellows and whites, and you will end up covered in turmeric. Day two is Indian formal — red and gold are welcome, and the evening by the sea gets cool, so bring a layer.
I've never been to an Indian wedding. Help?
You need to do nothing but turn up and join in. Everything is explained as it happens, and someone will always take you by the elbow and tell you where to stand.
Can I bring someone?
Your invitation says who it is for. If you are unsure, ask us — it is never awkward.
Are children welcome?
Very. There will be more of them than you expect.
