Chapter one

How we got here

A queue for bad coffee, four years of airports, and one very direct question.

Our story

Two passports, one kitchen,
a great many arguments about chai.

One

We met in a queue for coffee that neither of us thought was very good. Smriti said so first. Joey, who had been thinking it for ten minutes, decided on the spot that this was the most agreeable person he had ever met.

Two

What followed was four years of two time zones, a shared calendar colour-coded by whose turn it was to fly, and an ongoing dispute over whether tea should be boiled with the milk. (It should.)

Joey learned to eat food at the heat it was meant to be eaten. Smriti learned that the Dutch will, without embarrassment, put chocolate sprinkles on buttered bread and call it breakfast.

Three

Somewhere in there, both families started asking the same question at the same time. So: Mumbai, in December, for two days — everyone who got us here, in one room.

— Smriti & Joey
